What this tool is

Skynet Uploader is a personal command-line tool built and operated by one individual creator, Kenn Bosak, for his own use on his own computer. Each day he records a short video for his YouTube channel, and this tool automates the repetitive step of uploading that video — with its title, description, and custom thumbnail — to his own channel through the official YouTube Data API v3. It is not a public service: it has no other users, is not distributed to anyone, and runs only on the operator's own machine.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (upload only): the tool uses the YouTube Data API v3 videos.insert method (resumable upload) and the thumbnails.set method to publish the operator's own videos to his own channel, Kenn Bosak on YouTube, home of his show Not Another Bitcoin Podcast.
  • Google Drive (read-only): when the operator chooses, the tool reads a video file from the operator's own Google Drive (drive.readonly scope) solely so it can upload that file to YouTube. It never modifies or deletes Drive files.

Those are the only two Google API scopes the tool requests: youtube.upload and drive.readonly. It collects no analytics, stores no data obtained from the APIs, and shares nothing with any third party. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
